Flow
Flow is a digital clock that highlights walnut rings to mark the passage of time. The figures, drawn by luminous reliefs, are the result of Woowood© technology from Steven Leprizé Workshop. A unique fusion between traditional crafts and innovation.
Dimensions: L.1350 x H.500 x D.160 mm Walnut materials, Woowood, Arduino, motor, LED


It was a pleasure to participate in PROTOTYPE. We enjoyed being surrounded by talented artisans and designers, and we had many meetings at the show. We look forward to exploring the opportunities that flow from it."
Steven Leprizé
Founder of Atelier Steven Leprizé
