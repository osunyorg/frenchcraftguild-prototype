Papier peint Rayonnant
This project investigates the potential of utilizing traditional block-printing techniques to create functional patterns for wallpaper. In addition to its aesthetic appeal, it examines electrical conductivity and radiant heating, thereby introducing innovative perspectives for design and craftsmanship. The project was developed with the support of the Bettencourt Schueller Foundation.
