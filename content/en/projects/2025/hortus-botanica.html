Emerging from the recollection of the orchard and the joy of harvesting, the HORTUS BOTANICA series presents radiant figurative forms. These still lifes merge two artistic disciplines, glassblowing and chasing, to produce distinctive and limited works, harmonizing technical skill with visual poetry.  

  - Botanica Péponida lamp, 2025, limited series, chiseled brass and blown glass, about 15 cm x 18 cm. Made in collaboration with glassmaker Florian Harling. - Botanica Mirabella lamp, 2025, limited series, chiseled brass and blown glass, about 20cmx15cm. Made in collaboration with glassmaker Florian Harling.
