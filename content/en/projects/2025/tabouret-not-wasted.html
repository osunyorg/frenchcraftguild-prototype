The NOT WASTED series examines the capacity of furniture to foster environmental consciousness in the user. Each stool is crafted from the shavings produced during the carving of its base, held together by a natural binder composed of earth starch and water, resulting in an inert, biodegradable, and purposeful object.  
Materials: Branch, solid wood and revalued wood chips Dimensions: L30 x H50 x D20 cm Weight: 6 kg
