Ta.tamu

The Ta.Tamu chair exemplifies an innovative approach focused on reducing material usage. Utilizing virtual prototyping techniques such as topology optimization, generative design, simulation, and life cycle assessment, it is engineered for folded printing-a world first that merges technical ingenuity with resource efficiency. 
Material: polyamide (PA 2200) Weight: 3.9 kg Dimensions (folded): H. 340 × W. 615 × D. 540 mm Dimensions (unfolded): H. 800 × W. 635 × D. 590 mm
