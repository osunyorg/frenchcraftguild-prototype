MEMORIAE is an elegant jewelry box featuring a bowl with a turned and openwork porcelain neck. Its two-tiered lid, embellished with an ebony jeweled button set in brass and safeguarded by a porcelain cage, unveils a secondary compartment lined with cork and leather, capable of accommodating sixteen rings. A central handle grants access to the core of the box: a flame-patinated earthenware bowl lined with leather.      

Custom-made storey jewelry box in openwork porcelain, sandstone, leather, brass and ebony
