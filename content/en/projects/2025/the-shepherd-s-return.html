The SHEPHERD'S RETURN is the culmination of two years of research into the contemporary applications of French wool.

Created in partnership with Traille and Laulhère, this prototype emphasizes the structural and tactile properties of wool, providing a straightforward, sustainable, and respectful approach to transformation.
