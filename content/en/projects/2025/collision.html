The COLLISION series is founded on a protocol that connects graphic intention with three-dimensional creation. Utilizing illustrations from his sketchbook, Samuel Latour carves wood on a lathe through the technique of eccentric turning. It examines the repetition, variation, and arrangement of patterns within the specified confines of the cylinder.
