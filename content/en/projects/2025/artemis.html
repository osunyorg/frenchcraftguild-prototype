The ARTEMIS chair reimagines the  Bergère du débotté,  crafted around 1775 and attributed to Georges Jacob. Crafted from solid pear wood and enhanced with lacquer accents by Atelier Maury, it conjures a botanical realm through its verdant tones and selection of fruitwood. Its contours, reminiscent of deer antlers, adeptly explore the relationship between form and ornamentation.    
Solid pear wood, with lacquered canvas inlays by the Maury workshop. Dimensions: 70 x 70 x 90 cm Weight: 15 kg
