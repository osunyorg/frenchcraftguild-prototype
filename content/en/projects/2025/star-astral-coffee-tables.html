Envisioned as a harmonious pair, the ÉTOILE and ASTRAL tables form a seamless and sculptural arrangement. Each tabletop features a central aperture with a mirror, which reflects a 3D-printed design, creating an illusion of boundless depth. Marquetry, achieved by inverting the wood grain, interacts with light and texture, creating a nuanced conversation between technique and aesthetics.    
Small one dimensions: 845 x 675 mm; H: 335 mm Large one dimensions: 1150 x 800 mm; H: 420 mm Weight: 30 kg for both (15 kg small, 20 kg large) Material : Black tinted Sycamore. Marquetry made with different shades of black. White lacquered nylon 3D printing. Mirror
