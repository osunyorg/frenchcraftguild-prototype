Goudron de pin
Thomas Noui presents a solution derived from oleoresin extracted from charred pine trees, enhanced with soot, which merges aesthetic appeal with effective sealing capabilities. Adaptable to ambient temperature and suitable for various surfaces, this material draws inspiration from the Japanese tradition of urushi lacquer, merging artisanal heritage with technical innovation.
