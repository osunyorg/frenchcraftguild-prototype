Lamp Revolution

The REVOLUTION lamp, meticulously crafted from black stoneware, is inspired by Art Deco and pop culture. Its interchangeable modules facilitate the development of diverse configurations and the modification of the rotation angle, while its energy-efficient LED lighting provides adjustable intensity to establish a range of atmospheres.
