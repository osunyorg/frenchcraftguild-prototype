Fannylaure merges artisanal techniques with industrial processes to produce laminated glass panels featuring straw marquetry. The glass is tempered and subsequently baked with the marquetry, thereby safeguarding it from discoloration, detachment, and wear, which paves the way for new applications, including outdoor use.    

Patented innovation


' First of all, thank you for coming up with such an event, thank you also for your curiosity, which has fostered connections; for the intelligence of your vision; for the dedication you have shown and continue to show; for your professionalism, which was evident from the very start of the project and was so clearly on display at the exhibition; for your attention to both the big picture and the smallest details; and for the thoughtfulness that characterised our exchanges, which built trust and allowed ideas to flourish. And what efficiency! The result was magnificent. Well done once again; I was delighted to be part of this first edition, the initial positive impact of which I can already see, and thrilled to have shared this adventure with you. '
Fanny-Laure
Cabinetmaker, straw marquetry artist
