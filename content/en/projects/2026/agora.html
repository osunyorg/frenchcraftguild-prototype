AGORA is presented as a three-dimensional work in which a constellation of points of light unfolds across a concave canvas. Precise control of the light output constantly renews the viewer's perception, offering an ever-changing visual experience. This prototype thus opens up new avenues for application, at the crossroads of architecture, design, art and wellbeing.
Hand-woven, linen, wool and optical fibre Designer and cabinetmaker: MsurM, Lukas Sabbe Dimensions: 100 x 100 cm Weight: 6.5 kg  


‘What a success! Well done to you all!! A big thank you for the excellent organisation. I absolutely loved this event and the discussions I had over the two days. I'd very much like to repeat the experience in 2027, with some new avenues of research.’
Lyse Drouaine
Artist and textile designer
