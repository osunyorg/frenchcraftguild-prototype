VERA UBC is a generative tool with ultra-low power consumption. Fully autonomous, the device - which consumes less than 10 watts - produces unique formal compositions every six minutes. These are displayed on an E-Ink screen, without any additional energy consumption. This project forms part of an approach to creativity that is both technological and sustainable.  


' This system embodies our ethical and responsible approach to AI. It represents a vision of generative design that rejects instant gratification: the machine becomes an autonomous explorer of our creations, mapping out a new realm of possibilities to give rise to new pieces crafted in the workshop .'
Henri Danzin
Cofondateur de Jaùh
