The MONOLITH wall light, originating from the  Terre de Verre (Glass Earth)  research initiative, elevates glass dust, a recycling byproduct that is frequently overlooked and discarded. By merging the expertise of ceramics and glassmaking, Wilfried Becret transforms this material into durable, aesthetically pleasing, and ethically produced pieces that are entirely recycled and recyclable.   
Monolith light application: 55 x 20 x 6 cm - 6 kg   With the support of the  Fonds pour les Ateliers de Paris  and the City of Paris' B ureau du Design, de la Mode et des Métiers d'art .
