FILAE converts light into matter via additive manufacturing. The filament, infused with miscanthus fibers, is arranged flat like thread on a modern loom, resulting in delicate pendant lights. The efficient use of materials and minimalist design unveil a soft, luminous, and naturally golden presence, where light engages with the botanical material.  

Manufactured in 3D printing FDM (melted wire deposit), with the novifil PLM-20 loaded with miscanthus fibres grown in Seine-et-Marne (77)
