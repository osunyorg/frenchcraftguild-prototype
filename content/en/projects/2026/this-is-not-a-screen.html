With this piece, created entirely from soft or rigid textiles, Lily Latifi explores the material’s structural capabilities, its self-supporting nature and its potential joints. The work fosters a close dialogue between traditional craft techniques and digital processes, art and industry. The ‘cloud’ motif varies subtly from one panel to the next, depending on whether it is digitally engraved or hand-printed.
