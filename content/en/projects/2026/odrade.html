Odrade
ODRADE, a prototype 3D-printed ceramic lamp, exemplifies Lumierrante's proficiency in merging artisanal craftsmanship with industrial technologies. The ceramic is crafted with meticulous precision, enabling multi-material interlocking, which includes the LED system and blown glass diffusers, resulting in an object that is both technical and poetic.  
Lenamelled sandstone amp, aluminum, glass, LED system Dimensions: 40 x 40 x 200 cm Estimated weight: between 30 and 40 kg


"It was a great experience, which taught us a lot and brought us, and already several projects are underway following the show."
Jessica Boubetra
Co-founder of Lumierrante
