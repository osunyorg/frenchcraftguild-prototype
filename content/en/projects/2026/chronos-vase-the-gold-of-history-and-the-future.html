With 'CHRONOS', Daumet presents a ceramic vase designed as an engraved fresco depicting the history of gold. The piece is enhanced by the application of high-purity 18-carat white gold. Through this creation, the house brings together artisanal craftsmanship and innovation in materials science, whilst exploring contemporary issues relating to resource conservation.

The piece is enhanced by the application of high-purity 18-carat white gold.

Through this creation, the house brings together artisanal craftsmanship and innovation in materials science, whilst exploring contemporary issues relating to resource conservation.
