The artist intricately intertwines a poignant conversation between substance and recollection. By layering 420 stoneware and porcelain pellets vertically, she reconstructs a fictional geological core, serving as both an archive of time and a metaphor for contemporary identity. This work examines the imprints and relationships we create within the tapestry of existence. Suspended by a thread.     

Porcelain, stoneware, and wire • One-of-a-kind piece • 30 x 200 cm
