With ÉPROUVETTES, Jean-Baptiste Sénéquier reinterprets a Boulle chest of drawers from the Palace of Versailles by exploring the potential of 3D printing.

By pushing the software to generate as many structures as possible, the manufacturing process itself becomes a creative act, revealing the machine's traces on the final object.

Prototypes played a key role here in validating the technical feasibility of this experimental approach.
