This marine sediment, obtained from port dredging, is converted by the Brest-based company Gwilen into a low-carbon construction material.

Inspired by the natural process of diagenesis, it is composed entirely of minerals, devoid of cement or resin, and is well-suited for the creation of customized and sustainable projects that harmonize technical performance with environmental stewardship.
