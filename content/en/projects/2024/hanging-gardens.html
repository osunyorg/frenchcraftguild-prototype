This shoulder set conceals a miniature universe: the "Gardens" brooch, from which the "Primary Forest" brooch distinctly emerges. Crafted from an assortment of hand-dyed Japanese papers for the foliage, it is supported by a hand-shaped and assembled brass "suspension bridge" structure, which is then patinated, resulting in a prototype that is both delicate and structured.

Brooch Primary forest : feathers, foam agate, chrysoprase, titanium, lacquered brass leaves, blackened silver. Stones cut on work. Prototype Suspended Bridge  : Directed by S.Mart Grenoble Alpes & Université Sarah Bensrhir Frédéric Vignat. This prototype is presented with the support of the Banque Populaire Foundation. Winner of the Desirable Futures Award, competition organized by Francéclat in 2024.
